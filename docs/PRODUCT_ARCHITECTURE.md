## Digital Brand

### Konfydence Challenge

Digital scenario-based decision game.

Customer-facing editions:

1. School Edition
2. University Edition
3. Family Edition
4. TravelSafe Edition
5. Workplace Edition

One core game engine (rules, card format, scoring) powers all five; only the scenario decks, tone, and packaging differ per edition, since that's where the buyer-facing value and relevance actually sit.

## Do Not Fragment Further

The five editions above are the approved ceiling. Avoid creating additional separate customer-facing products for:

- Finance
- HR
- Procurement
- Faculty
- Executives
- Department-specific curricula within an existing edition

Use tags internally to segment content within an edition, but keep the customer-facing product architecture to these four.


## Self-service CoMaSy pilot (October 2026)

Free four-week pilot for every organisation, up to 20 participants and 12 scenarios across campaigns. Named verified administrators invite participants and author their own content, or choose existing company scenarios. Overview shows status and evidence gaps before detailed reporting. Continuing requires an explicit onboarding and per-seat agreement; there are no paid-pilot tiers or automatic conversion. See COMASY_CUSTOM_SCENARIOS.md and CUSTOMER_HOSTED_ARCHITECTURE.md. Customer-hosted identity/system adapters are proposed; the current hosted database still stores participant records. Optional aggregate-summary download sends no data to the vendor.
