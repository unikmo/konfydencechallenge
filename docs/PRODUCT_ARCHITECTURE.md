## Digital Brand

### Konfydence Challenge

Digital scenario-based decision game.

Customer-facing editions:

1. School Edition
2. University Edition
3. Family Edition
4. TravelSafe Edition
5. Workplace Edition

One core game engine (rules, card format, scoring) powers all five; only the scenario decks, tone, and packaging differ per edition, since that's where the buyer-facing value and relevance actually sit.

## Do Not Fragment Further

The five editions above are the approved ceiling. Avoid creating additional separate customer-facing products for:

- Finance
- HR
- Procurement
- Faculty
- Executives
- Department-specific curricula within an existing edition

Use tags internally to segment content within an edition, but keep the customer-facing product architecture to these four.
